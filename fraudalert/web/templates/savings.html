Savings

Monthly saving as a control loop. SP is your savings target; PV is the savings this month is heading for. Income and fixed expenses are known in advance and taken off up front (feedforward); card spending is corrected by feedback against a plan. OUT is advice, since you're the one who acts: how much you can still spend per day. HI when the projection is more than 15% short of the target, HIHI when you'd spend more than you earn.
